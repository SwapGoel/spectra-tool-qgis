about=A spectral profiler for any multi-band raster (multispectral, hyperspectral, SAR stacks, time series, indices...). Click the map to plot every band of a pixel, or drag a box to plot its average spectrum.

Key Features:
- **Works with any raster:** Wavelengths are read from ENVI headers, GDAL/NetCDF/HDF metadata or band names; with none available the plot uses band index.
- **Auto-fitting axes:** X and Y follow the data's own min and max; pan, box zoom, wheel zoom and typed limits hold the view until Reset.
- **Multi-Point / Multi-Layer:** Compare many pixels in one layer, or one pixel across every visible layer.
- **Point / Area:** Read a single pixel or average a dragged rectangle.
- **Interactive legend:** Click to hide a curve, Shift-click to solo it.
- **Light and dark themes:** Follows QGIS and the system appearance automatically.
